# AI PM Portfolio

A portfolio of hands-on AI product management projects, built to show I can **lead AI work in regulated financial services environments** — payment rails, digital asset infrastructure, banking compliance, and enterprise SaaS — not just describe it.

## Positioning

Most product managers calling themselves "AI PMs" stop at the surface. This portfolio goes deeper: shipping AI under real constraints — model risk management (SR 11-7), auditability, accuracy guarantees, security, and cost. And it does so in a specific domain: **banking and fintech infrastructure**, where AI decisions touch payment rails, digital asset custody, and federal regulatory compliance. These are the rooms where AI is hardest to do well, and where credible PMs are thinnest on the ground.

## Domain knowledge

This portfolio is built for the intersection of **AI systems** and **banking/fintech infrastructure**. That means understanding:

- **Payment rails** — RTP (Real-Time Payments), FedNow, Fedwire, ACH, wire transfers, and intrabank book transfers. How money moves, at what speed, under what regulatory rules, and where AI adds value at each layer.
- **Digital asset services** — Institutional custody (cold storage, MPC), buy/sell execution (OTC desk, brokerage), staking (PoS validators, yield, slashing risk), and tokenization. The regulatory landscape: OCC Interpretive Letters, FinCEN Travel Rule, qualified custodian requirements.
- **Banking compliance & governance** — SR 11-7 model risk management, ECOA/Regulation B fair lending, BSA/AML obligations, and how each applies when AI is in the decision loop.

## The four capabilities this portfolio proves

1. **Translate AI capabilities into business outcomes** — make evidence-based model and approach decisions, not vibes-based ones. Applied to payment processing and banking use cases.
2. **Direct technical work** — scope and guide RAG systems, agents, and MCP integrations credibly enough to lead an engineering team.
3. **Define and measure AI quality** — build evals and speak to AI quality with numbers, not impressions.
4. **Manage AI risk** — govern AI for regulated environments: model risk management, digital asset compliance, fair lending, and security.
