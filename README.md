AI PM Portfolio
A portfolio of hands-on AI product management projects, built to show I can lead AI work in regulated industries — banking, enterprise, and SaaS — not just describe it.
Positioning
Most product managers calling themselves "AI PMs" stop at the surface. This portfolio is built to go deeper: shipping AI under real constraints — auditability, model risk, accuracy guarantees, security, and cost. These are the rooms where AI is hardest to do well, and where credible PMs are thinnest on the ground.
The four capabilities this portfolio proves

Translate AI capabilities into business outcomes — make evidence-based model and approach decisions, not vibes-based ones.
Direct technical work — scope and guide RAG systems, agents, and integrations credibly enough to lead an engineering team.
Define and measure AI quality — build evals and speak to AI quality with numbers, not impressions.
Manage AI risk — govern AI for regulated environments: model risk management, fairness, security, and compliance.
